mostly papers whose submitted title is not the published one (`Learning Sorting and Classification with POMDPs` was
published as `Learning Sorting and Decision Trees with POMDPs`); others are titles dblp spells differently

records (8 in 1997, 15 in 1998) keep no abstract, the coverage report's missing-abstract count. Every one of the
91 abstract claims names the official page, the capture's timestamp, and that the abstract is a submission-time
abstract; the coverage report's scope line counts them.
