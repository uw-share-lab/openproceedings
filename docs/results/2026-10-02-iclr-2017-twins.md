## The rule (`openreview_v1.link_twins`, rule 6)
A copy is a record from a non-main submission listing. Its twin is the record from the main-track submission
listing with the same dedup title key. When several records share that key, the `_bibtex`-named one is the twin.
Otherwise there must be exactly one. Both records stay records, since two forum ids are two submissions
(dedup-rules §Never merge). Each gets one `twin` claim (source `openreview_v1`) naming the other's id.

On the cache this links 53 copies: 18 by `_bibtex` and title, 35 by title alone. Two conference notes each have
two copies, so 51 conference records are linked. That makes 104 records in all.

The pairs, by the `content.venue` each side carries (read from the built records' status evidence):

| Copies | Linked by | Copy's `content.venue` | Twin's `content.venue` (track) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | `_bibtex` and title | Submitted to ICLR 2017 | Submitted to ICLR 2017 (main/rejected) |
| 34 | title | ICLR 2017 Invite to Workshop | ICLR 2017 Invite to Workshop (workshop/unknown, conference listing) |
| 1 | title | (none) | Submitted to ICLR 2017 (main/rejected): one of the 18's twins, which so has two copies |

The 34 `Invite to Workshop` copies have 33 distinct twins: one conference note has two copies.
